How it works

Five steps from slab to second chance.

Build your order

Add each card to your cart. Include the cert number so we can match it on arrival.

Ship it to us

Pack the slab securely and send it. Shipping is on you, so choose a tracked service.

Packed and sent home

What cracking does not do

It does not change your card and it does not promise a higher grade. A regrade can come back higher, the same, or lower. Resubmission rules belong to each grader, so check theirs first.

Why regrade

Why a second look matters

A graded label changes how a card is seen. It adds exclusivity, heritage and legacy to the piece.

The label carries weight

A grade on a slab shapes demand, trust and value. For collectors, it can feel like the card's identity.

It is still a human judgment

Graders compare a card to a standard by eye. Collectors know the same card can see different outcomes.

The market is concentrated

PSA's parent company, Collectors, has also bought Beckett. That leaves collectors with fewer places to turn.

Source: ESPN

Second chances belong to collectors

If you believe your card earned more, you should have a path to ask again. That path starts with getting the card safely out of the slab.

Before you crack

  • Check the grader's current resubmission policy.
  • Remember that the old grade no longer travels with the card.
  • A new grade can come back lower.

About
